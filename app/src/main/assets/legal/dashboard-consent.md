RouteMock works fully without this. If you allow it, this phone connects out to a dashboard address you enter.

Uploaded: a pseudonymous label, phone model, Android version, your consent receipt, and session summaries containing title, app version, selected speed, and duration.

Not uploaded: your real location, coordinates of sessions, or device identifiers such as serial numbers.

Remote control is off until you turn it on here. Even then, each requested simulation start must be approved by you on this phone. The local Stop button always works, with or without a connection.

Whoever runs the dashboard holds the uploaded data, and their retention applies. Withdrawing this consent stops uploads and remote requests on this phone at once and asks the dashboard to revoke access. If this phone is offline, that request is kept, encrypted, and sent when the app next has a connection. Until then the dashboard still shows this phone as connected, although this phone will not act on anything it sends. Deleting data already uploaded must be done in the dashboard.
