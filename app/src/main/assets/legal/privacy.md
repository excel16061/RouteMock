Privacy Notice (version 1)
DRAFT pending legal review. This is not legal advice.

Stays on this phone: routes you save, your consent receipt, and the real location shown on the map. Real location is held in memory only, is not logged, and is not saved.

Sent to the map provider (OpenFreeMap by default): the map areas you view and your IP address.

Sent to the routing provider (Valhalla on the FOSSGIS server by default): the start and destination pins, and your IP address, only when you tap Calculate road routes. If a pin is at your real location, that location is what gets sent.

Sent to your dashboard: nothing, unless you separately turn on dashboard pairing.

Retention: data on this phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. RouteMock does not control how long providers keep their server logs; see each provider's privacy policy.

To stop: tap Stop in the app or notification. You can withdraw consent, view, and export your receipt in the More section. Withdrawing mock-location consent stops any running simulation.

RouteMock does not ask for your legal name, signature, ID, or date of birth.
