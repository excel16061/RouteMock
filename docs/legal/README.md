# Consent documents

The text users see is in `app/src/main/assets/legal/`. All four are **drafts pending legal
review**, written to describe what the app actually does. They are not legal advice, and
consent does not act as a liability shield or replace Android's permissions. No compliance
with any particular law is claimed.

Changing a document's text requires users to accept it again, because the app compares
content hashes.

## Questions for a legal reviewer

1. Do the terms need a governing law or dispute clause, and for which places?
2. If the app is distributed, who is the data controller for each dashboard operator?
3. Do the documents meet the transparency rules of the places it will be used, such as the
   GDPR or California law, and is any further notice needed?
4. Is accepting by checkbox suitable for these purposes where it will be used?
5. Is there a minimum age to state? None is collected or checked today.
6. Is the warranty statement effective, and does consumer law limit it?
7. How do OpenFreeMap, FOSSGIS, and OpenStreetMap terms apply if the app is distributed?
8. What applies if test data is used in academic research: ethics review, data retention?
9. How long should consent records and audit logs be retained?
